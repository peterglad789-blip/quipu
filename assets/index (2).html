NASA SPACE APPS 2026 · INTERPLANETARY SURVIVAL GUIDE

Can you go, do science, and make it back alive?

On Mars there is no GPS, no rescue, and oxygen runs out. QUIPU ties data from NASA missions together, like the knotted cords Andean peoples used to record information, so an astronaut can plan a walk in Jezero Crater and know, at every step, whether they will make it home.

    Sound on Mars · Perseverance microphone NASA DATA

    On Mars, low sounds travel at ~240 m/s and high-pitched sounds at ~250 m/s (Earth: ~343 m/s). How long would your shout take to reach your partner?

    Illustrative audio SIMULATION: on Mars the thin CO₂ air makes sound much quieter, muffles high pitches, and low and high tones arrive at slightly different times.
